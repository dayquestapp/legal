# Day Quest - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Day Quest mobile application
("Day Quest", "the app", "we", "us") handles information.

Day Quest is designed to keep your data on your device and in your own
iCloud account. We do not run our own servers for your content, we do not
sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across apps or
websites. The app does send us two narrow categories of technical data,
described below: automatic crash reports, and feedback you choose to
submit.

## Your content stays yours

All content you create in Day Quest - quests, subtasks, journal entries,
timers, themes, statistics, and settings - is stored locally on your
device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, Day Quest also syncs this content to the
private iCloud database attached to your Apple ID, so your data can move
between your devices and survive a reinstall. This sync uses Apple's
CloudKit service:

- The data goes to your private iCloud storage, not to us. We have no
  access to it and no ability to read it.
- Apple encrypts this data in transit and at rest under Apple's terms.
- You can disable iCloud for Day Quest in iOS Settings at any time.

If iCloud Backup is enabled, your device backup may also include Day
Quest data. That backup is between you and Apple.

## Crash reports and diagnostics

Day Quest uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash-reporting service operated
by Google, so we can find and fix crashes. If the app crashes, a report
is sent to Crashlytics containing:

- A technical stack trace of the crash
- Device model, operating system version, and app version
- Device state at the time of the crash (such as available memory and
  orientation)
- A randomly generated per-installation identifier used to group crash
  reports (a Firebase installation ID)

Crash reports do not include your name, email address, quests, journal
entries, or other content you have created. The installation identifier
is not your advertising identifier and is not used to track you across
apps; we use crash data solely to diagnose and fix problems.

Google processes this data on our behalf. For details, see Google's
privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Firebase
Crashlytics data disclosure
(https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Feedback you choose to send

Day Quest has an optional in-app feedback form. Nothing is sent unless
you write a message and tap submit. When you do, we receive:

- The message you wrote
- App version, device model, and iOS version

If you submit a problem report (as opposed to a feature request), the
submission also includes a diagnostic log: a technical report containing
the app's recent internal error messages and the size of its local
database. In rare cases an error message may mention the name of an item
it relates to (for example, a quest name that failed to save). You can
choose to send a feature request instead if you prefer not to include
diagnostics.

Feedback is stored in Google Firestore, a database service operated by
Google, and is used only to improve the app and respond to problems. It
is not linked to your identity unless you include identifying details in
your message.

## What we do not collect

Day Quest does not collect, store, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any account identifier
  (Day Quest has no accounts)
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, or device files
- Usage analytics or behavioural data
- Advertising identifiers

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking of any
kind. The only third-party services in the app are Apple (StoreKit,
CloudKit, notifications) and Google Firebase (Crashlytics crash
reporting and Firestore feedback storage), as described above.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Day Quest offers in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple's App
Store using StoreKit. When you make a purchase:

- The transaction is handled entirely by Apple.
- We never see, receive, or store your payment information.
- Apple provides Day Quest with a receipt confirming whether you have an
  active entitlement, so the app can unlock premium features. This receipt
  contains no personal information.
- Subscription management (cancellation, refunds, billing) is handled by
  Apple in the App Store account settings on your device.

For details on how Apple handles purchase data, see Apple's privacy policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Day Quest schedules local
notifications on your device to remind you of quests and timers. These
notifications are generated entirely on-device and are not sent through
any server.

## Children

Day Quest is suitable for general audiences and is not directed to
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children; the only data we receive (crash reports and voluntary
feedback) is technical in nature and not used to identify anyone.

## Your rights

Your quests, journal entries, and other content never reach us, so there
is no content for us to access or hand over.

Crash reports and submitted feedback do reach us. If you would like the
feedback you submitted or the crash data associated with your
installation deleted, contact us at the email below and we will delete
it. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for 90 days. You can also
delete all local Day Quest data at any time by uninstalling the app, and
remove synced data via iOS Settings > iCloud > Manage Storage.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
California and have questions about your rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or
the CCPA, you can contact us at the email below.
